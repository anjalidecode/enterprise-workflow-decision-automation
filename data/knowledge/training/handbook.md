# Employee Training & Skill Development Handbook

## Employee development process

Employee development starts with a clear view of current skills, role
requirements, and any performance-related development signals. The training
workflow identifies skill gaps, matches catalog courses, applies structured
training policy, and recommends or enrolls suitable learning. Structured policy
and tools are authoritative; this handbook is explanatory only and never
overrides policy validation.

## Training request procedure

Training requests may ask for skill-gap analysis, course recommendations, or a
training plan. Requests should identify the employee. The workflow retrieves
employee data, skills, training history, and catalog options through tools, then
produces an evidence-based recommendation.

## Manager approval

Manager approval is required when selected training exceeds the manager cost
threshold defined in structured policy. Higher-cost specialized programs may
also require HR approval. Until approval is recorded, enrollment write actions
must not run.

## Course selection

Course selection considers skill coverage, level, format, duration,
availability, cost, and prerequisites. Prefer active courses with available
seats that address prioritized skill gaps. Alternatives may be listed when more
than one suitable option exists.

## Prerequisites

Prerequisites must be satisfied before enrollment. Missing prerequisite skills
or incomplete prerequisite courses block enrollment. Policy validation is the
authority for prerequisite checks.

## Training completion

Completed training is recorded in training history. Completions inform future
skill-gap analysis and reduce duplicate recommendations for skills already
covered. Incomplete or withdrawn history remains visible for context.

## Development planning

A training plan documents recommended courses, target skills, and enrollment
status. Plans support employee growth and manager coaching. They do not trigger
termination, demotion, salary change, or other high-impact employment actions.
