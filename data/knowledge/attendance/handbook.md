# Attendance Handbook

## Attendance expectations

Employees are expected to be present for scheduled working days, arrive on time,
and record attendance accurately. Structured attendance policy and tools are
authoritative; this handbook is explanatory only and never overrides policy
validation.

## Late arrival procedure

Arrivals after the standard start time beyond the grace period are recorded as
late. Repeated late arrivals may trigger an attendance warning and manager
visibility. Employees should notify their manager when delayed.

## Absence reporting

Planned absences should be requested through leave workflows. Unplanned absences
must be reported to the manager as soon as practical. Consecutive unreported
absences escalate for manager or HR review.

## Attendance correction procedure

Missing or incorrect attendance records should be corrected through HR with
manager confirmation. Workflows that detect missing records may block automated
actions until data is complete.

## Manager review process

Managers review attendance warnings, repeated lateness, and low attendance
percentages. Review tasks document findings and recommended coaching; they are
not automatic disciplinary actions.

## Escalation procedure

Serious attendance issues—such as attendance below the escalation threshold or
consecutive absences beyond policy—pause high-impact actions for human approval.
After approval, the system may create a review task and notify stakeholders.
Disciplinary measures remain outside automated workflow actions.
