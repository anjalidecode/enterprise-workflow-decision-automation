# Employee Onboarding Handbook

## Onboarding process

Onboarding prepares a new hire for productive work. The workflow verifies identity
and employment documents, applies onboarding policy, plans orientation tasks,
requests equipment and system access, and notifies stakeholders. Structured
policy and tools are authoritative; this handbook is explanatory only.

## Document verification

Mandatory documents typically include identity proof, address proof, education
certificate, employment contract, and tax document. Missing or unverified
documents block onboarding actions until resolved. Do not treat handbook text as
a substitute for document verification tools.

## HR orientation

HR orientation covers company policies, benefits enrollment, and compliance
acknowledgements. Orientation tasks are created only after document and policy
gates pass, or after human approval for privileged-access cases.

## Manager responsibilities

Managers introduce the new hire to the team, confirm role expectations, and
sponsor equipment and access requests. Manager assignment is required before
onboarding can proceed.

## Employee responsibilities

Employees submit required documents promptly, complete orientation tasks, and
acknowledge workplace policies. Incomplete submissions delay equipment and access
provisioning.

## Equipment

Standard equipment may include a laptop, monitor, and headset. Equipment
requests are recorded through write tools and must not be claimed complete unless
the tool result confirms success.

## System access

Standard access often includes email, collaboration tools, and source control.
Privileged access such as production administration or secrets vault entry
requires human approval before provisioning.

## Approval procedures

Privileged system access pauses the workflow for human approval. Until approval
is recorded, no task creation, equipment request, access request, or completion
notification write actions should execute.
