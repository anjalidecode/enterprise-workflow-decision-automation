# Performance Management Handbook

## Performance review process

Performance reviews compare employee results against agreed goals and KPIs for a
defined review period. The workflow retrieves records and goals through tools,
applies structured performance policy, and produces a recommendation. Structured
policy and tools are authoritative; this handbook is explanatory only and never
overrides policy validation.

## Goal setting

Goals should be specific, measurable, and agreed at the start of the review
period. Goal achievement is calculated from recorded actuals versus targets.
Completed, partially completed, and unmet goals are reported as evidence, not as
automatic disciplinary findings.

## KPI evaluation

KPI evaluation covers quality, delivery, project completion, and stakeholder
measures recorded for the period. KPI results inform strengths, development
needs, and skill-gap analysis. Do not infer sensitive personal characteristics
from performance data.

## Manager responsibilities

Managers set goals, give timely feedback, and review performance
recommendations. Manager review tasks document coaching and development next
steps. Managers do not use this workflow to automatically terminate, demote, or
penalize an employee.

## Employee development

Development recommendations identify skill gaps and learning focus areas.
Development plans and performance improvement plan recommendations are tasks for
manager/HR follow-up. They are not automatic sanctions.

## Feedback

Feedback should be specific, evidence-based, and tied to goals and KPIs.
Positive reviews recognize strong achievement. Mixed results should include both
strengths and development areas. Serious concerns require documented review.

## Performance improvement process

When goal achievement falls below policy concern or escalation thresholds, the
workflow may recommend a manager review and a performance improvement plan task.
The plan is a recommended development path. It must not impose disciplinary
punishment, salary reduction, demotion, or termination.

## Approval requirements

High-impact employment decisions require human approval. Serious performance
concerns pause automated writes until a manager or HR reviewer approves. After
approval, the system may create a review task, create an improvement-plan
recommendation, notify stakeholders, and update review status. Disciplinary
measures remain outside automated workflow actions.
