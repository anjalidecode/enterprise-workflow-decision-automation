# Employee HR Services Handbook

## How employees submit HR requests

Employees and managers submit HR service requests through the Employee HR Services
workflow. Requests are classified into supported categories such as leave balance,
attendance inquiry, employment documents, policy information, benefits, profile
updates, payroll routing, training information, onboarding status, recruitment
status, and general HR support. Structured tools and policy are authoritative;
this handbook is explanatory only.

## Document request process

Employment certificates, experience letters, and employment verification letters
are handled as simulated document requests. A service ticket records the document
type, employee, and status. Documents are not emailed automatically in this
platform phase.

## HR support process

General and unsupported requests create an HR service ticket and may be routed to
HR. Employees receive a ticket number and next-step guidance. Support tickets do
not bypass authorization or policy controls.

## Benefits information

Benefits overviews, enrollment pointers, and related FAQs are provided from
curated knowledge. Benefits answers explain process and where to look; they do
not change enrollment elections or compensation.

## Payroll support routing

Payroll questions and salary discrepancies are routed to HR/payroll support.
The platform does not implement a payroll system and must not disclose another
employee's salary. Payroll issues create a high-priority ticket and escalate.

## Profile change process

Profile updates such as phone number or address changes create an HR service
request and may require approval. Sensitive profile and payroll fields are never
changed automatically by the workflow.

## Confidentiality rules

Employees may access their own leave, attendance, and onboarding status.
Managers and HR roles may access team or organization-scoped records when
organization context allows. Cross-employee personal, payroll, performance, and
private HR records must not be disclosed without appropriate authority.

## Escalation process

Payroll issues, unauthorized sensitive-data requests, and high-impact profile
changes escalate to human review or HR. Escalation pauses automated writes and
records the approval requirement.

## Service ticket lifecycle

Tickets move through open, in_progress, escalated, resolved, and closed states.
Updates are idempotent tool writes. Seed tickets demonstrate open, resolved, and
escalated examples for testing and demos.
