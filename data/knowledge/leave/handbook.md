# Employee Annual Leave Handbook

This handbook explains how employees request annual leave. Numeric eligibility
rules are defined in the structured leave policy. This document is guidance only.

## Leave request guidance

Employees should submit a leave request with an employee id, the number of days,
and a start date. Annual leave is the default leave type. Requests are reviewed
against available leave balance and employment status before any action is taken.

## Manager approval for long leave

Leave requests of 5 or more days require manager approval. Longer leave needs
additional review because of team coverage. The workflow records a pending
human-approval state and does not update leave balance until a manager approves.

## General leave process

The leave process is: identify the employee, retrieve leave balance, apply the
leave policy, analyse impact, decide, validate, then execute approved actions
or return a rejection or approval-required response.

## Policy concepts

Available leave requirement means requested days must not exceed the remaining
annual balance. Active employment is required. A single request has a minimum
of 1 day and a maximum of 15 days. These numeric limits are enforced by the
structured policy engine, not by this handbook.
