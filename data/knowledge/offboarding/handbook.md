# Employee Offboarding & Exit Management Handbook

## Resignation process

Employees submit a resignation or exit request that identifies the employee,
exit type, resignation date, and requested last working day. The offboarding
workflow coordinates preparation tasks. Structured policy and tools are
authoritative; this handbook is explanatory only and never overrides policy
validation.

## Notice period

Notice period requirements are defined in structured offboarding policy. Valid
notice allows preparation to continue. Insufficient notice or missing notice
details may block or escalate the request depending on policy rules.

## Exit checklist

A standard exit checklist covers manager review, HR review, asset return,
knowledge handover, exit interview scheduling, and access-revocation requests.
Checklist items track completion status and blockers. The checklist coordinates
work; it does not itself terminate employment.

## Asset return

Assigned assets such as laptops, monitors, access cards, and mobile devices must
be returned or recorded for return. Outstanding assets create return tasks and
warnings. Asset-return writes are controlled tool actions with idempotency.

## Knowledge transfer

Knowledge handover covers projects, documents, and knowledge areas owned by the
departing employee. Incomplete mandatory handover creates handover tasks and
warnings. Handover does not authorize privileged access changes.

## Exit interview

Exit interviews are scheduled as simulated coordination events. Scheduling
records a request; it does not finalize employment status or compensation
outcomes.

## Access revocation

System-access revocation is handled as a request, not an automatic privileged
revocation. Privileged access revocation requests and employment-status changes
require human approval before write actions run. The workflow must not terminate
employment or revoke privileged access without approved human review.

## Manager responsibilities

Managers review exit timing, confirm handover coverage, and approve or escalate
when policy requires manager review. Managers do not bypass structured policy
controls.

## HR responsibilities

HR confirms notice compliance, checklist completeness, asset and handover
status, and approval needs for privileged access or employment-status changes.
Final termination and compensation decisions remain outside automated workflow
actions.
