# Recruitment Handbook

## Recruitment process

Recruitment follows a structured workflow: understand the job, retrieve candidates,
analyze and score profiles against requirements, apply recruitment policy, then
recommend shortlist, review, or reject outcomes.

## Candidate evaluation guidelines

Evaluate required skills first. Preferred skills improve ranking but do not replace
missing required skills. Experience, education, and location alignment contribute
to a transparent weighted score.

## Interview procedure

Interviews are scheduled only after a candidate is shortlisted and human approval
is recorded for high-impact hiring actions.

## Approval procedure

Final shortlist and interview scheduling require human approval. Policy and scoring
tools remain authoritative; handbook guidance is explanatory only.

## Interview scheduling policy

Schedule interviews for approved shortlisted candidates and notify both the
candidate and the recruiter. Do not claim an interview was scheduled unless the
scheduling tool succeeds.
